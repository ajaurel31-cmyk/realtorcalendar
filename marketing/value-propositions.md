## 1. AppSumo Listing Field

*For the "Value Propositions / Differentiators" form field:*

**One-time price, not a subscription.** Most real estate content tools charge $29–$99/month. Realtor Content Calendar is a single purchase with lifetime access — saving agents $350–$1,200/year.

**Full month of content in 60 seconds.** One click generates a balanced calendar of listings, educational posts, personal branding, and engagement content. No prompting, no brainstorming, no blank-screen paralysis.

**Export anywhere.** PDF for printing and hanging on the office wall. CSV for Google Sheets or scheduling tools. ICS for Google Calendar and Outlook. Three formats, zero friction.

## 3. Pitch Deck / Investor Slides

### Slide: The Problem

- 1.5M+ active real estate agents in the US alone
- 78% say social media is important for their business (NAR)
- #1 barrier: **"I don't know what to post"** — content planning paralysis
- Agents spend 5–10 hours/month planning content, or they don't post at all
- Existing tools cost $30–$100/month — high churn, low retention

### Slide: Our Solution

- **One-click content generation** — full month of posts in <60 seconds
- **214+ agent-specific templates** across 4 balanced categories
- **Auto-personalized** — name, brokerage, city, market fill into every caption
- **Multi-format export** — PDF, CSV, ICS for any workflow
- **Zero infrastructure** — runs entirely in-browser, no backend costs

### Slide: Why We Win

| Differentiator | Us | Competitors |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing model | One-time ($39–$117) | $29–$99/month |
| Time to value | 60 seconds | Hours of setup |
| Content scope | 214 templates, 4 categories | Varies, often generic |
| Privacy | No accounts, no servers | Requires login + data |
| Distribution cost | $0 (static site) | Servers, auth, DB |

### Slide: Business Model

- **One-time purchase** via AppSumo, Gumroad, or direct
- **$0 marginal cost per user** (no backend)
- **Expansion revenue**: seasonal template packs, Canva integration, scheduling tool partnerships
- **LTV multiplier**: team/brokerage licenses (2x and 3x tiers)
