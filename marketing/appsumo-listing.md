### Product Description

Tired of staring at a blank screen every morning, wondering what to post?

You know consistent social media wins clients. But coming up with fresh content ideas for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok — while running showings, handling closings, and managing clients — is exhausting.

Most content tools charge $29–$99/month. Realtor Content Calendar gives you everything for a one-time price.

**HOW IT WORKS**

**Step 1:** Enter your name, brokerage, and city. Every post auto-fills with YOUR details.

**Step 2:** Click "Generate My Month." Your calendar fills instantly with a balanced content mix.

**Step 3:** Customize, rearrange, and export. Edit any post, drag to reorder, download as PDF, CSV, or ICS.

**WHAT'S INSIDE**

214+ post templates across 4 categories, balanced for maximum engagement:

🟢 **Listing Posts** (55 templates) — Just listed, open house, price drop, just sold, coming soon, under contract, virtual tours

🔵 **Educational Content** (55 templates) — Market updates, buyer tips, seller tips, neighborhood spotlights, mortgage info, maintenance tips

🟡 **Personal Branding** (52 templates) — Your origin story, client testimonials, behind the scenes, milestones, community involvement

🟣 **Engagement Posts** (52 templates) — Polls, quizzes, this-or-that, hot takes, fill-in-the-blank, guess-the-price

**FEATURES**

- One-click calendar generation with smart 30/30/20/20 content mix
- Visual monthly calendar, color-coded by post type
- Drag-and-drop to rearrange posts across dates
- Full caption editor — edit text, switch platforms, add private notes
- Auto-filled hashtags — platform-specific sets + your custom tags
- Best posting times suggested for each platform
- Image suggestions — every post tells you what photo/graphic to pair
- Posting cadence options — 3x, 4x, or 5x per week
- Brand customization — your name, brokerage, city, and colors throughout
- Export to PDF (printable wall calendar), CSV (Google Sheets), ICS (Google Calendar)
- Works on desktop, tablet, and phone
- No software to install — opens instantly in your browser
- 100% private — everything saved locally, no accounts, no servers

**WHO THIS IS FOR**

- Solo agents who want to post consistently but don't have time to plan
- New agents building their brand and online presence
- Experienced agents leveling up their social media game
- Teams and brokerages looking for a simple content planning tool

## ADDITIONAL: FAQ (pre-seed in Q&A section)

**Do I need a Canva account?**
No. Realtor Content Calendar generates ready-to-post captions with image suggestions. You bring your own photos — each post tells you exactly what type of image to use.

**Is this AI-generated content?**
No. All 214+ templates are written by real estate marketing professionals. They're designed to sound like a real agent, not a chatbot.

**Can I use this for multiple months?**
Yes. Click "Generate My Month" each month for a fresh calendar. Templates are randomly selected so each month is different.

**Does it post to social media for me?**
Not yet — it generates the content and calendar. You copy captions and post them yourself, or export to CSV and import into your scheduling tool (Buffer, Later, Hootsuite).

**What happens if the product shuts down?**
The app runs entirely in your browser — no servers required. Even if the website goes offline, any exported PDFs/CSVs/ICS files are yours permanently.

**Can I customize the templates?**
Absolutely. Every post is fully editable. Change captions, toggle platforms, add private notes, swap in a different template with one click.
