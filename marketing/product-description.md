### FAQ

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. It opens instantly in your browser. Works on Mac, PC, phone, tablet — anything with a web browser.

**Can I edit the posts?**
Absolutely. Every post is fully editable. Change the caption, switch platforms, add your own notes.

**What if I don't like a suggested post?**
Hit "Regenerate" and it swaps in a different template from the same category. You have 214+ to choose from.

**Does it work for my market?**
Yes. You enter your city and market during setup, and every template auto-fills with your local details.

**Can I print it?**
Yes. Export to PDF for a printable wall calendar, or CSV to import into Google Sheets.

**Is my data private?**
100%. Everything is saved in your browser's local storage. No accounts, no servers, no one else can see your content.
